About Me

Nikos is a Computer Science graduate student at Columbia University. A team player, passionate about solving complex problems and building scalable systems. With professional experience as a Machine Learning Engineer and a degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering, he aims to bring innovation to every project he works on. He holds an Integrated Master of Engineering from the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National Technical University of Athens, having graduated in the top 5% of his class of 400 students. He was also in the top 1% of students in Greece in the Greek National Exams. A recipient of the Proud of Youth Scholarship from HelleniQ Energy and the Aegean Shipping Management Scholarship from the Union of Greek Shipowners, Nikos has a proven track record of delivering under pressure.

IntelliQ
The goal of this project was to develop an "intelligent" app for creating and answering questionnaires. The admins can login, create them, see the answer history and the relevant statistics while the users can answer the respective questionnaires. Light and dark themes are included as well as the ability to copy the link of the created questionnaire and share it. My account is ntua-el19647 in this project. View Project
Evaluating LLM Resilience to Adversarial Data Poisoning
Designed a method to assess and quantify the resilience of RoBERTa LLM in sentiment analysis against adversarial data poisoning by applying influence function-based perturbations, which revealed a model accuracy drop of up to 15% under structured adversarial attacks. Generated text embeddings using BERT and performed dimensionality reduction within Databricks to facilitate sentiment analysis. View Project
