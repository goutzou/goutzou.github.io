About Me

Nikos is an ex-Blockchain Data Scientist Intern at Ripple and a Computer Science graduate student at Columbia University. A team player, passionate about solving complex problems and building scalable systems. With professional experience as a Machine Learning Engineer and research experience in Software Systems, he aims to bring innovation to every project he works on. He holds an Integrated Master of Engineering from the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National Technical University of Athens, having graduated in the top 5% of his class of 400 students. He was also in the top 1% of students in Greece in the Greek National Exams. A recipient of the Proud of Youth Scholarship from HelleniQ Energy and the Aegean Shipping Management Scholarship from the Union of Greek Shipowners, Nikos has a proven track record of delivering under pressure.
